My Approach

Understanding, not memorising

The same habits, applied to every strand.

Understanding Before Formulas

You'll understand what a formula is for before you memorise it. Method without meaning is what falls apart under exam pressure.

Working, Not Just Answers

Most marks live in the working. I teach you to set out a solution so a marker can follow your reasoning ,and so you can find your own mistakes.

Real-World Connections

Where it helps, I show a concept as a picture or a real example. When the idea clicks, the formula stops feeling arbitrary.

Exam & Assessment Technique

How to read a question, show enough working for full marks, and check an answer actually makes sense.

A note on what I don't do

I don't do the homework for you. I make sure you understand the topic well enough to solve the next problem on your own ,which is what the test will actually ask of you.
